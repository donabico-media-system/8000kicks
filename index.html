OUR HERITAGE

Built By Family. Rooted In 8,000 Years Of Botanical History.

Bernardo and his grandmother, backed by generations of textile expertise in Portugal, set out to revolutionize modern footwear. They selected industrial hemp—the world's most sustainable fiber—and re-engineered it into a waterproof, lightweight, and indestructible material.

Hemp requires 5x less water than cotton, uses zero pesticides, and actively sequesters carbon from the atmosphere during growth. Every pair of 8000Kicks shoes carries this eco-friendly heritage forward into the modern age.

Why Industrial Hemp Over Synthetic Leather?

Superior natural performance meets environmental restoration.

💨

Microscopic Breathability

Hemp fibers feature a porous open-weave cellular structure. Air circulates continuously to vent out internal foot heat, keeping your feet dry and cool all day long without synthetic chemical coatings.

🛡️

3× Tensile Strength

Industrial hemp is recognized as the toughest natural fiber on earth. Highly resistant to tears, abrasions, and harsh weather conditions while breaking in comfortably over time.

🌿

Anti-Odor & Antimicrobial

Hemp fibers naturally repel moisture retention, hindering bacterial and fungal growth. Eliminates shoe odor at the source without relying on chemical sprays.
